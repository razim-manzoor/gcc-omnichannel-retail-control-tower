Executive Control Tower Architecture Brief

Enterprise Decision Engineering & Semantic Model Blueprint

1

Commercial Problem Solved

Eliminates inventory starvation in high-footfall GCC luxury flagships (The Dubai Mall) by dynamically flagging overstock in regional hubs (Yas Mall Abu Dhabi) and simulating markdown price elasticity before approving margin-dilutive sales. Rebalances physical capital across the Emirates to preserve full-margin retail sales velocity.

2

Key Business Metrics Engineered

GMROI ($> 3.00\text{x}$)

Annualized gross margin return per AED of inventory capital invested, linking velocity to margin.

Price Elasticity ($\% \Delta Q = -\epsilon \times \% \Delta P$)

Modeled category volume lift vs. margin surrendered to test discount feasibility.

Working Capital Release

Locked liquidity converted to cash through cleared inventory, measured against margin sacrifice.

3

Enterprise Technical Stack

  • Kimball Star Schema: 2 Fact Tables (POS Sales Transactions + Daily Inventory Snapshots) and 3 Conformed Dimensions (Dim_Date, Dim_Store, Dim_Product) joined on integer surrogate keys.
  • Tabular Semantic Model: C# TOM automation via Tabular Editor 3 deploying 6 curated measure folders into _Measures repository.
  • Advanced DAX: Semi-additive inventory snapshots via LASTNONBLANK, zero bi-directional filters, and isolated parameter harvesting (Markdown_Scenario).
  • Verification Harness: Full analytical invariant and referential integrity assertions pre-validated in DuckDB.
