Executive Architecture & Data Governance

Kimball Star Schema: Grounded in 115,008 POS transactional sales and 53,808 daily store inventory snapshots across 8 GCC retail nodes. 1-to-many single-direction relationships joined strictly on surrogate integer keys.

Prescriptive Rebalance Logic: Cross-evaluates store-level Sell-Through Rate (STR %) against Weeks of Supply (WOS). Inter-mall transfers protect full-price gross margin without resorting to margin-diluting markdowns.

Price Elasticity Engine: Dynamic microeconomic formulas (\(\% \Delta Q = -\epsilon \times \% \Delta P\)) project category unit lift against baseline profit hurdles to verify breakeven viability and working capital return on investment.
